Calisthenics Hierarchy

Four ways
to train.

Calisthenics can be trained in different ways. Each style develops a different physical quality — strength, explosiveness, endurance or control.

01
🏋️
Maximum Strength

Weighted

Add external resistance to bodyweight movements to develop greater strength and power.

Weighted Pull-up Weighted Dip Weighted Muscle-up
02
⚡
Explosiveness & Movement

Dynamic

Fast, explosive and rotational movements that challenge coordination, power and body control.

Muscle-up 360 Bar Spins Flips
03
🔥
Work Capacity

Endurance

High repetitions and longer training efforts designed to build muscular and cardiovascular work capacity.

High-Rep Pull-ups Push-ups Dips Circuits
04
🗿
Isometric Strength

Static

Isometric holds that demand exceptional strength, body control, stability and technique.

Front Lever Planche Human Flag 90° Hold
